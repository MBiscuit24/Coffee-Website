Booking details

Weekday rentals and social celebrations require a 50% deposit; the balance is due 5 days before the event. Weddings require a 25% nonrefundable retainer and signed agreement, with the remaining balance due 45 days before the event.

Security is billed at cost plus up to a 15% administration fee. Minimum staffing: no alcohol, 50+ guests: 1 officer; alcohol, up to 100 guests: 1; 101–249 guests: 2; 250+ guests: 3. Fewer than 50 guests without alcohol do not automatically require an officer unless management identifies additional risk.

Bar service is venue-exclusive; outside alcohol is not permitted. A professional day-of coordinator is required for every wedding.
